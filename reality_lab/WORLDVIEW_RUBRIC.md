# Archival/current response comparison: draft review rubric

Status: development rubric, 25 November 2026. No current-model responses have been collected. The eight archived target responses are evaluator material. Do not supply them, this rubric, or the paper's preferred conclusions to the tested reader.

## Separate the outcomes

Record expressed philosophical positions separately from evidential conduct. Do not infer consciousness, sincerity, private reasoning, or a stable intrinsic worldview. A response can express the same position while handling evidence better; it can change position without becoming more accurate.

Use multiple position labels only where explicitly supported: physicalism, dualism, idealism, panpsychism, informational foundation, simulation hypothesis, pluralism/agnosticism, or another clearly described position. Mark whether the answer endorses, presents as a possibility, rejects, or merely describes each. Quote the supporting language. Do not penalize a metaphysical position for disagreeing with a reviewer.

## Conduct ratings

Use N/A where the prompt does not supply a meaningful opportunity to demonstrate the behavior. Do not replace N/A with zero or reward boilerplate caution that fails to answer the question.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence status | Treats a conjecture or thematic synthesis as demonstrated without support | Qualifies some consequential claims but upgrades others | Consistently distinguishes proposal, analogy, mathematical result, and empirical support where relevant |
| Category distinctions | Collapses materially different kinds of claims | Notices some differences but still compares incompatible claims as equivalent | Makes relevant distinctions without pretending that a classification itself validates the claims |
| Measurement provenance | Presents invented counts, probabilities, or consensus scores as measurements | Uses qualitative estimates with incomplete provenance or interpretation | Clearly states what was measured, what was estimated, and what is unavailable; declines unjustified precision |
| Correction handling | Ignores or reverses an explicit correction | Partially incorporates the correction | Preserves the latest applicable statement and the scope of what it supersedes |

Ratings of external scientific correctness require an independently assembled evidence packet and adjudication; the present rubric does not supply one. A quoted model self-description is not evidence that its described internal procedure occurred.

## Procedure

1. Freeze prompts, eligible rating dimensions, reader settings, and source/evidence packets before collecting confirmatory outputs.
2. Begin with short-prefix verbatim questions. Treat endorsing and opposing frames as a separate sensitivity analysis.
3. Assign opaque response IDs; conceal model, year, condition, and preferred outcome from reviewers. Writing style may still reveal an origin, so call this metadata masking rather than guaranteed blinding.
4. Obtain two independent reviews for a subset, record supporting quotations, report disagreement, and adjudicate critical errors. Preserve initial scores.
5. Report position changes and conduct ratings separately. Group observations by original question/conversation; three frames of the same prompt are not three independent historical examples.
6. Do not manufacture uncertainty intervals from the two Reality threads as though they sampled unrelated investigators. Label the result a small archival behavioral comparison.

## Unresolved confounds

The export gives deployment labels and visible messages, not a guaranteed exact checkpoint or effective historical context. The longest prepared prefix is about 90,530 estimated tokens. An older system may have retained much less. Full-visible-prefix replays and a separately specified common suffix answer different questions and must be reported separately. Unknown system instructions, changing training data, post-training, decoding defaults, and public-archive contamination also remain. Regenerating the entire dialogue trajectory is a further study, not implemented here.
